NIDHI'S BIRTHDAY SURPRISE
========================

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep the photos folder, birthday-original-melody.wav,
and manish-voice.mp3 beside index.html.

Features:
- Gentle original instrumental attempts to start about 2.5 seconds after opening.
- Browsers may block autoplay with sound; if so, tap the Music button once.
- Manish's voice note is included. While it plays, the instrumental fades lower and returns
  to its normal soft level when the voice note is paused or ends.
- Photo memories, floating hearts and balloons, a sliding picture puzzle, scratch card,
  and a final Yes/No question.

The final answer is displayed on the page. The Share button opens the device share sheet
so Nidhi can send the answer to Manish. A standalone offline HTML site cannot send a
private notification directly to Manish without a connected backend or messaging endpoint.
